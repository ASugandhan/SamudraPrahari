### max_area_frac was fixed from 0.4 → 0.85 (would have inflated the bad news)
A first run used `max_area_frac=0.4`; but **9/51 sctd GT boxes exceed 0.4 of their (small,
tight) crop** — real shipwrecks up to 0.78 — so the guard made them unhittable by
construction, dragging sctd recall to a false 0.65. Raising to 0.85 (still rejects the
degenerate near-whole-tile blob; largest real GT = 0.78) restored sctd to 0.882. ai4 boxes
are tiny (maxfrac 0.03), so their 0.69 is genuine AE misses, not a guard artifact.

## Deviations / honest notes

  (an sctd wreck at 0.78 of its crop) stays hittable — see the guard-fix note above.
- The real crops never went through T1.8 (no `valid_mask`) → treated all-valid; the mask path
