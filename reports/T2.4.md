## Deviations / honest notes
- **The `anomaly_only` pool is large (4037, ~21/image)** — that's the accepted-weak AE (T2.1)
  firing diffusely, NOT a fusion bug. It is the raw UNKNOWN pool and MUST be pruned downstream
  (T3.3 shadow verification, T4.2 open-set scoring) before anything is shown to an operator.
- **Duplicate-rate caveat (transparency):** 0.032 is redundant-candidates ÷ ALL fused candidates;
  that denominator is inflated by the anomaly_only noise. Per hit-object the picture is worse
  (~137 redundant vs ~227 GT hits) — residual anomaly blobs adjacent to detected objects. The
  centre-inside merge cut this (both: 10→117, dup 0.041→0.032); the rest is the weak-AE tail.
  The literal ≤5% target is met; the real reduction needs a stronger anomaly screen upstream.
- YOLO-alone recall here (0.749, object-level union at IoU≥0.1/centre-inside over ALL classes
  incl. ai4 tiny) differs from the mAP50 headline (0.947 SCTD) — different metric, both honest.
