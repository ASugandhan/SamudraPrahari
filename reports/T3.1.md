## Root cause + fix (why v2 should clear the target)
v1 tiled training with `keep_bg=0.15` (kept only 15% of pure-background crops), but the test
uses ALL crops → the model rarely saw pure seabed in training and floods the test with false
positives. **Fixed:** notebook now trains with `keep_bg=0.5` (aligns train/test background rate),
and exports fp16 (R11). Retrain → expect FP to drop and IoU to rise toward the 0.40–0.44 range.
